March · The Wedding

The Girl I Ignored

Before the wedding, you tried to talk to me first. I'll be honest — I barely gave you any attention. I was too busy being a distracted best man to notice what was standing right in front of me.

Wedding Day

The Dance

Then the day came, and you talked to me like we'd known each other for years — easy, warm, a little flirty. After the photos, we danced. You held my hand and pulled me in. I can't dance — I still can't — but I tried anyway, just to keep dancing with you.

Then some guy pushed in to dance with you, and out of nowhere I felt jealous. Jealous, over a girl I'd met that day. So I talked myself out of it: she's just friendly, she's like that with everyone, not just me. I decided you weren't the one for me — someone that playful could easily get bored and move on. I was sure of it.

(I was, obviously, very wrong.)
That Night

Looking for You

Before I left, I searched for you everywhere just to say goodbye. Couldn't find you. The next day I got sick and couldn't come to see you again — you sent me a photo of what you were planning to wear and asked if I'd come. I spent that whole day imagining you out there, laughing, dancing, talking to everyone else. Tried to forget about you. It didn't work.

The Weeks After

We started messaging — day after day. Somewhere in there, all the distance I'd been trying to keep just quietly disappeared.

Your Trip

The Almost-Confession

Instagram, Late Nights
